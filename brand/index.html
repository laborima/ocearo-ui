Brand guidelines

The Ocearo brand.

How the Ocearo name, mark and colours are built, and how to use them. For articles, videos, partners and contributors.

Logo

The logo is the word OCEARO in which the O is the compass of the 3D view and the A is a bow splitting the horizon. Use the dark version on dark backgrounds and the light version on light ones.

02

Construction

Every part of the logo comes from the app itself.

  • The O is the compass of the 3D view, tilted 60° in perspective like the ring that floats around the boat, with its bezel, its twelve graduations fading towards the back and the own boat at its centre.
  • The boat heads 045, towards the rest of the name: white jib, Ocearo-blue mainsail, standing in its centreline plane.
  • The A is the bow: two blades meeting at the stem, the horizon line in Ocearo blue passing behind and cut where they touch.
  • The letters are Geist Regular in capitals, tracked at 0.34 em, like the labels of the instrument panel (SOG, HEADING, TWS).
  • Small sizes: at 48 px and below the compass is shown flat, face-on, with the two sails, so it stays sharp.

Colours

06

Interface principles

The look of the app and of everything around it.

  • Calm first. A dark, quiet screen; only what needs attention lights up.
  • One accent. Ocearo blue marks the own boat and the actions. Everything else is white or grey.
  • Colour means something. Red, violet, orange and green follow the COLREG and navigation-light conventions, everywhere.
  • Numbers you read from afar. Large values, small spaced labels, tabular figures.
  • Real pictures only. Screenshots and videos come from the app with real data, never mock-ups or stock photos.
